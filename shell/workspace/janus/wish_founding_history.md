# Wish Company Founding History and Establishment Process

## Overview
Wish is a mobile e-commerce platform that connects consumers with merchants, primarily focusing on offering products at discounted prices. The company was originally known as ContextLogic Inc.

## Founding Timeline

### 2010 - Company Establishment
- **Founders**: Peter Szulczewski (CEO) and Danny Zhang (CTO)
- **Original Name**: ContextLogic Inc.
- **Location**: San Francisco, California, USA
- **Initial Concept**: The company started as a B2B platform, using machine learning to connect Chinese manufacturers with Western consumers

### 2011-2012 - Early Development
- The founders met while working at Google and later at other tech companies
- They identified an opportunity in cross-border e-commerce, particularly between China and the US
- Initially focused on helping Chinese merchants sell to Western markets

### 2012 - Launch of Wish Mobile App
- Launched the Wish mobile shopping application
- Pivoted from B2B to B2C (business-to-consumer) model
- Introduced the "Wish" brand name for their consumer-facing platform
- Focused on mobile-first shopping experience

### 2013-2015 - Rapid Growth
- Expanded product categories beyond initial offerings
- Grew merchant network, primarily from China
- User base expanded rapidly, reaching millions of active users
- Secured multiple rounds of funding from investors

### 2015-2019 - Scaling and Expansion
- Continued international expansion
- Introduced various features to improve user experience
- Faced challenges with product quality and delivery times
- Invested heavily in machine learning for product recommendations

### 2020 - Public Listing
- Went public via SPAC (Special Purpose Acquisition Company) merger
- Listed on NASDAQ under the ticker symbol "WISH"
- ContextLogic Inc. became the public entity name

## Business Model
- **Platform Type**: Mobile-first e-commerce marketplace
- **Key Feature**: Algorithm-driven product recommendations
- **Pricing Strategy**: Discounted prices, often significantly lower than traditional retailers
- **Merchant Base**: Primarily Chinese manufacturers and sellers
- **Revenue Model**: Takes commission on sales, advertising fees from merchants

## Key Facts
- Headquarters: San Francisco, California
- Founders Background: Both Peter Szulczewski and Danny Zhang have strong technical backgrounds
- Original Focus: Cross-border e-commerce between China and Western markets
- Differentiation: Mobile-first approach with personalized product feeds

## Challenges and Evolution
- Product quality concerns from consumers
- Long shipping times due to international sourcing
- Increased competition from other e-commerce platforms
- Regulatory scrutiny in various markets
- Shift toward more diverse merchant base beyond China
